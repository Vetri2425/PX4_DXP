1 · CSV → mission integrity — before the rover moves

These three catch the failures that put paint in the wrong place. They cost minutes at a laptop and save a re-survey.

The load sequence (verified 2026-07-25 — do not improvise)

1. POST /api/path/upload                      (multipart "file")        -> saved
2. GET  /api/path/{name}/preview                                        -> control_points
3. POST /api/path/{name}/plan-and-stage   body: {"source": "{name}"}    -> mission_id
      ^ OMIT origin_gps — the engine adopts the CSV's own geo_origin
4. POST /api/path/load-to-controller      body: {"mission_id": "stg_…"}
5. POST /api/mission/start                body: {"mission_id": "stg_…", "auto_origin": false}

Never send path_name to /start after step 4. It re-loads from disk as LOCAL_NED and silently discards the surveyed placement. A failed start leaves the mission loaded — wait for RTK and retry, do not re-stage.

★ = the product's biggest unproven claim  ·  ★★ = suspected existing defect

Why 2.2 matters most. The "1–2 cm on arcs" figure is extrapolated, not measured for a CSV curve. The validated 1.46 cm RMS comes from the R=1.5 m built-in arc, and it is budget B — driven versus its own path. Measured against the surveyed shots, the same curve sat mean 1.96 / max 3.40 cm. For a product whose entire value is "the line is where the survey says it is", that is the number that has never been proven in the field.

3 · How to grade — the honest rulers

Run python3 tools/analyze_mission.py <bundle> per bag, then judge on the budgets below. They are separate questions and one number must never stand for another.

#QuestionMeasureTarget
AWas the plan the right shape, in the right place? planned /path vs the surveyed lat/lon≤ 2 cm
BDid the controller follow its own plan? pose vs raw /path (report §7)≤ 2 cm RMS
CWhere did the rover physically go? raw GNSS vs the surveyed lat/lon — this is the verdict ≤ 2.5 cm, no drift along the line
DDid the paint land there? physical re-survey of the mark no log can answer this — only a tape or a rover shot

Never quote /rpp/debug[0] as accuracy. It is cross-track against the conditioned path — the controller grading its own homework — and it structurally hides dropped vertices. /spray/debug[5] is nozzle cross-track carrying an uncalibrated offset; also not tracking error.

Budget D is the one to grab while you are on site. Paint is perishable — it gets walked over, weathered, and driven on. A re-survey of today's marks closes the only error budget no log will ever answer, and it also yields the nozzle offset (band centre vs the surveyed line) that bug B4 is waiting on.
