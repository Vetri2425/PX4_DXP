## 3. What was cleared

**Planning.** All 7 runs published a **bit-identical** path: start within **1 mm**
of surveyed station A, end 10 cm past B (deliberate extension), 3.169 m. Identical
across both stagings despite the 4.76 m origin move — cross-session placement
determinism holds here (relevant to open bug A7).

**The controller.** Deviation from its own path, marking phase: **0.67–2.98 cm RMS**.

**The analyser.** The §9 TRAVERSAL verdicts were independently recomputed and
matched on all 7 runs, including the subtle case: run 122855 *started* at 83 % but
drove back and marked the full line, and was correctly scored 64/64. The three
partial runs really did begin part-way along; 122756 never opened the valve.
**The recorder/analyser is not at fault.**

## 4. The finding

The paint lands at **path position + EKF lateral error**. That error behaves
completely differently between the two blocks.

| Block | Run | mark @start | mark @end | EKF error @end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WENC=0 | 122512 | −8.39 cm | **+0.65** | +0.12 cm |
| WENC=0 | 122855 | −1.79 cm | **+0.49** | +0.67 cm |
| WENC=1 | 121153 | +5.11 cm | **+4.46** | −3.76 cm |
| WENC=1 | 121323 | +0.58 cm | **+4.34** | −2.88 cm |
| WENC=1 | 121448 | +0.76 cm | **+3.15** | −1.71 cm |

- **WENC=0 — the error is TRANSIENT.** Initial errors of 8.4 cm and 1.8 cm both
  decay to **half a centimetre** by the end of the line. The start condition stops
  mattering, so runs coincide.
- **WENC=1 — the error is PERSISTENT and ONE-SIDED.** Every run ends **3–4.5 cm
  left**. The two that started near zero walked left at **1.27–1.51 cm/m**; 121153
  started 5.1 cm left and simply stayed there for 3 m.

**And the estimator hides it:**

| Block | EKF self-report (RMS) | ground truth (RMS) | understatement |
|---|---|---|---|
| WENC=1 | 1.29 cm | 3.55 cm | **2.75×** |
| WENC=0 | 2.19 cm | 3.41 cm | 1.56× |

Fusion improved the *reported* number by 40 % and left the ground truth unchanged.

### Mechanism isolated — by accident

The firmware review ([[wenc-fusion-code-review]]) named two candidates:

| # | mechanism | on an arc | on a straight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | heading-bias leakage `v·sin(ψ_err)` | drifts | **drifts** |
| 2 | missing lever-arm compensation `ω·d` | drifts | vanishes (ω≈0) |

**This session was a straight line, and the drift appeared anyway.** That is the
mechanism split the designed A/B's straight-line runs exist to make: it points at
**heading-bias leakage** and largely clears the lever arm.

Implied heading error from the walk rate: `sin⁻¹(0.013) ≈ **0.8°**`
(2026-07-25 arc data gave ~1.1°).

### Why the error cannot decay with fusion on

- **`EKF2_WENC_NOISE` / `_LAT_N` = 0.1** at ~50 Hz vs GNSS velocity 0.3 at 10 Hz
  → ~**45:1**, so the encoder owns ~98 % of the lateral-velocity weight. It asserts
  "zero lateral velocity in body frame", and body frame is defined by the biased
  heading — injecting lateral error continuously, always to the same side.
- **`EKF2_GPS_P_NOISE = 1.0`** floors the GPS observation noise at one metre
  (`gps_control.cpp:354`), so RTK's 1.5 cm fix is treated as a **1-metre sensor**
  and cannot pull the estimate back over 3 m.

Error is injected faster than it can be corrected, so it settles at an equilibrium
~4 cm left instead of decaying to zero.

## 5. Proposed fix, in order

**1 — Parameters only, no rebuild, via QGC on the Mac.** A/B these the same way,
do not just apply them.

| Param | Now | Proposed | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EKF2_GPS_P_NOISE` | 1.0 | **0.05** | let RTK actually be RTK — this is what stops the pull-back |
| `EKF2_WENC_NOISE` / `_LAT_N` | 0.1 | **0.35** | PX4 default; breaks the 45:1 dominance |
| `EKF2_WENC_GATE` | 3 | **5** | PX4 default; lets bad encoder obs be rejected |

**2 — Measure `EKF2_GPS_YAW_OFF`** (open bug **B1**). It is 180.0 — a round number,
assumed, never measured. Park on the surveyed line and compare the dual-antenna
heading against the true bearing **55.674°**. Predicted error **0.8–1.1°**. With
body-frame velocity fusion this is a position-accuracy parameter, not a display one.

**3 — Firmware (needs a rebuild).** Stop WENC refreshing
`_time_last_hor_vel_fuse` / `_ver_` (`ev_vel_control.cpp:203-204`). It currently
does, which structurally disables the EKF-GSF emergency yaw reset
(`gps_control.cpp:111`) — the estimator's own recovery from exactly this fault.

**4 — Lever arm** (`EKF2_WENC_POS_X/Y/Z`, mirroring `ev_vel.h:52-54`):
**deprioritised** by the straight-line result above.
